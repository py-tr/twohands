## Notes for the edit

- Something working is on screen from 0:00 (the live strip); a receipt by 0:16; the station pick by 0:26; the
  measured numbers by 0:55.
- Never show a party name or logo close-up: crops of vote rows only; rows are "Option NN" in the app.
- Never say "fraud" or "election" in the voice-over; say "tally sheet", "capture error", "official capture".
- Every rate in the voice-over is from the pre-registered 300 (app/data/headline.json); the pilot is not quoted as a
  rate. The receipt at 0:08 (202451B0100, TOTAL) is a single field from the 43-form pilot, confirmed by the certified
  count; the live numbers are read off the screen as they are on the day.
- Credit card at the end (2 s, small): "Tally sheets: INE PREP 2024, free-use terms. Not an official INE product.
  Rainfall ledgers: Rainfall Rescue, Hawkins et al. 2022, images linked not redistributed."
