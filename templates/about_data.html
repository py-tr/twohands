About the data

Where the live audit's numbers come from

{{ NOT_OFFICIAL }}

Sources

  • Images: the photos and scans of the presidential tally sheets (actas de escrutinio y cómputo de casilla, 2024) that INE publishes with its preliminary results program (PREP). Fetched from INE's public PREP URLs at most once per second, with the User-Agent {{ UA }}; a refusal (HTTP 429 or 403) pauses fetching.
  • Official capture: INE's PREP database (the keyed capture of each acta, cut of 3 June 2024).
  • Certified count: INE's Cómputos Distritales (cut of 8 June 2024). It covers the vote rows and the vote total, not sections 2 to 5 or 7. Its observations say whether the district council settled a form by comparison (the acta's figures were compared and kept) or by recount (the ballots were counted again).

What is stored

Only the privacy-masked page (names and signatures blacked out before anything is written or sent), downscaled to at most {{ page_kb }} KB, and the crops of disputed fields. The unmasked image exists only in memory while it is masked. Vote rows are named Option 01 to Option 15; no vote total is summed or shown.

How a form is read and labelled

An open vision model on the AMD Token Factory reads every field twice (digits and words) with the frozen prompt of the measured run; the reader's model id is recorded on every row and receipt. A field is a dispute when the model's digits and words agree with each other and differ from the official capture. Labels, on the vote rows and TOTAL of forms settled by comparison:

  • {{ LABELS.confirmed }}: the certified value equals the model's read and differs from the capture.
  • {{ LABELS.model_misread }}: the certified value equals the capture and differs from the model's read.
  • {{ LABELS.unconfirmed }}: everything else (sections the certified count does not cover, recounted forms, or a certified value that matches neither).

Receipts

Each dispute is a leaf of a Merkle tree (SHA-256, the scheme of the audit reports), one tree per state in audit order: a completed state has a final root, the open state a running root. A receipt is checkable offline with python -m twohands.verify_receipt.

Budget and pace

The worker spends at most ${{ '%.2f'|format(cap) }} a day on AMD and always leaves ${{ '%.2f'|format(reserve) }} for the site's live-read button; when the limit is reached it waits for the daily reset at 16:00 UTC. It shares one rate limiter with the live reads.

Separate from the measured results

The live audit skips every form in our pre-registered and measured samples, and its numbers never enter them: those are on Results, with their method. Status: Live audit.
