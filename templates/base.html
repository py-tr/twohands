Data. Tally-sheet images and the official capture: INE PREP 2024 (prep2024.ine.mx) and certified Cómputos Distritales 2024 (computos2024.ine.mx), consulted 2026-10-14, reused under INE's free-use terms (Libre-Uso-MX, SICEE). Images are shown only after privacy masking: the column with officials' names and signatures is blacked out before anything is stored. This is an independent demonstration of clerical capture quality; it is not an official INE product and does not represent a position of INE or any government.

Rainfall ledgers: Rainfall Rescue (Hawkins et al., Geoscience Data Journal 2022; github.com/ed-hawkins/rainfall-rescue; Met Office scans). Only our extracted values are shown; sheet images are linked at their public source. Every number on this site is listed with its source in docs/NUMBERS.md.
