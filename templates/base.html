Data and credits

Tally sheets and their official capture: INE PREP 2024 and certified Cómputos Distritales 2024, reused under INE's free-use terms (Libre-Uso-MX). Rainfall ledgers: Rainfall Rescue (Hawkins et al., Geoscience Data Journal 2022; Met Office scans), linked, not redistributed.
