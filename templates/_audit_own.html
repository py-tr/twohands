Bring your own data audit your own keyed capture against its paper, or request an audit

Bring the keyed data (a CSV) and the scans. You get back every field where the paper disagrees with the keyed data, ordered by evidence, each with its photo, for a person to decide. Download the sample keyed CSV to see the format, or request an audit.

Your own audit

The keyed file stays on this server for this audit only. Scans are privacy-masked in memory before anything is stored.

The keyed data

The scans

What you get

  • Every keyed field compared with the model's digits and its words, and with the paper's own arithmetic (rows sum to TOTAL, section 5 = section 7 = TOTAL).
  • Disputes order: strong when the model's digits and words agree and the keyed value breaks an identity the model's value satisfies; weak when the model saw a blank or its read breaks the arithmetic.
  • A crop of the masked page for each, and a "confirmed / not an error" mark for the auditor.
  • The audit report as CSV and as a printable page.
