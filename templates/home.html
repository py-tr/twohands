Touchless form capture with proof

The model reads. The paper decides.

Organisations pay people to key every paper form twice, because one keyer's mistakes cannot be found any other way. Many forms check themselves: amounts in words and in digits, rows that add up to a printed total, sections that must agree. Two Hands lets an open vision model on AMD GPUs read every field, then applies the form's own arithmetic. A form that passes every check is accepted with no human touch; anything else goes to a person with the failing field named.
